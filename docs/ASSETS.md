# Prime Path visual assets

## Identity

The supplied Prime Path Holdings logo informed the navy and bright blue palette and angular double-peak geometry. The trucking identity adds a road through the mark and the descriptor **TRUCKING & LOGISTICS**, with **PRIME PATH** retained as two words.

The final horizontal logo is included at full resolution as `public/media/logo-prime-path.png`. The website uses the final white-background artwork directly. The small vector favicon is a simplified geometric companion for browser tabs.

## Photography direction

The new imagery was created with the built-in image generator in reference-edit mode, using the new logo as the branding reference. The creative briefs were:

| Asset             | Direction                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `fleet-hero.webp` | Wide commercial fleet photograph at a Texas distribution facility in warm morning light. Show a white day-cab tractor and trailer, a white box truck, and a white high-roof cargo van together. Apply coordinated navy and blue Prime Path branding and preserve a clear view of every vehicle. |
| `day-cab.webp`    | White modern conventional day-cab semi with a short cab and dry-van trailer at a Texas commercial facility. Three-quarter view with visible company branding and realistic fleet proportions.                                                                                                   |
| `box-truck.webp`  | White medium-duty conventional-cab rigid box truck at a commercial dock. Three-quarter view, single rear axle, coordinated Prime Path branding, and natural daylight.                                                                                                                           |
| `cargo-van.webp`  | White high-roof commercial cargo van outside a Texas business facility. Three-quarter view with legible Prime Path branding and realistic natural light.                                                                                                                                        |

The optimized WebP files are included locally. These are brand concept images; substitute photographs of the actual operating fleet when available.

## Video files

- `fleet-loop.mp4`: 12 seconds; 1600 × 800; H.264; silent; a subtle continuous camera move over the fleet lineup.
- `fleet-film.mp4`: approximately 18 seconds; 1600 × 900; H.264; silent; the lineup followed by each vehicle class, joined with crossfades.

The motion pieces are edited still photography, not generated driving footage. `scripts/create-motion.py` reproduces both outputs with FFmpeg and preserves image proportions.
